## Try it

https://github.com/its-anantagarwal/peeknote/releases/tag/v1.0.0

## Features

- Quick note taking  
- Automatic note saving  
- Keyboard shortcuts and mouse movements to open and close the window  
- Notes history  
- Edit/delete notes

## How to use Peek Note

 - Download windows executable  
 - Run it  
 - To open the popup, move your mouse to the centre part of the top of your screen  
<img width="1917" height="362" alt="topPartOfScreen" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bca28973-d2b7-4742-82d5-3eeabad23fb5" />
 - Popup will slide down  
<img width="1917" height="297" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bee6fbdf-10dd-40d7-b4f2-3b5d1e9a950d" />
 - Click on the text box and write your note.  
 - You can click save, or move your mouse away from the window. This will cause the text to automatically save and the popup will slide back up.  
 - Click the plus button to create a new note. Your old note will be automatically saved.  
<img width="1917" height="297" alt="PlusButton" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6f50bb6f-9701-425a-b6c0-caf807131d0b" />
 - Click the save button to save the note  
<img width="1917" height="297" alt="SaveButton" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/98933bf4-6aa9-449b-abcb-8e1d0525495c" />
 - Click the history button to open the history menu  
<img width="1917" height="297" alt="feLnRdXKTu (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e94aa2d0-60ab-48ab-b946-e02c8beee9fd" />
History menu:  
<img width="1917" height="287" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/49f59020-40a8-4ed3-b476-c8b06da6d4d8" />
 - Click the back button to go back to the text field.    
Note history:  
<img width="1917" height="276" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ec5df44a-49df-4833-a9d8-e5e000cbfb5d" />
 - Double click a note to open it.  
 - Click on a note to select it, and then click on the Delete note button to delete it.  
<img width="1917" height="271" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/99a29e3d-dae4-43df-8ace-7d9bdcc1f5a1" />

 - Press the Quit button to close the program  
 - You can also use Ctrl + Space to toggle the hiding and showing of the window.  
 - You can use Esc to hide the window.
